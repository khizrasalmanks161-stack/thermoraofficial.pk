Meet Khizra Salman

At 17, after learning that many women miss school, work, and social activities because of period pain, I wanted to create a simple and comforting solution that people could use anywhere. That vision became Thermora, Pakistan's first self-heating patch brand, created to bring warmth and comfort to everyday life.
